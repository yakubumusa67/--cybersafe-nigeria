The Digital Threat Is Real

Cyber threats continue to evolve. Understanding the risks is the first step toward protecting yourself, your business and your digital identity.

🎣

Phishing

Fake messages and websites designed to trick people into revealing passwords, financial information or personal data.

HIGH RISK
💳

Online Fraud

Scammers use social engineering, fake offers and impersonation to target individuals and businesses.

HIGH RISK
🔑

Password Attacks

Weak or reused passwords can make accounts easier for attackers to compromise.

Daily Cyber Tips

  • Never share your passwords.
  • Always use Two Factor Authentication.
  • Update your applications regularly.
  • Do not click suspicious links.
  • Verify every email before opening attachments.
  • Use strong passwords.
  • Do not use public Wi-Fi for banking.
  • Lock your devices when not in use.
  • Backup important files regularly.
  • Install antivirus software.
  • Never share your OTP codes.
  • Use different passwords for different accounts.
  • Avoid downloading unknown files.
  • Keep your browser updated.
  • Always logout from shared devices.

Tips for a Strong Password

  • ✔ At least 12 characters long.
  • ✔ Include uppercase letters.
  • ✔ Include lowercase letters.
  • ✔ Include numbers.
  • ✔ Include symbols like @ # $ % & * !
  • ✔ Never reuse passwords.

Popular Nigerian Online Scams

Fake Job Offers

Be careful of job advertisements requesting payment before employment.

Investment Scams

If someone promises unrealistic profits within a few days, it is probably a scam.

Bank Verification Scams

Your bank will never ask for your PIN or password through WhatsApp or SMS.

Scholarship Scams

Always verify scholarship announcements before making any payments.
