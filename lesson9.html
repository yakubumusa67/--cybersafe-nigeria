Device Security & Privacy

⏱ Estimated Time: 20 Minutes

📱 Why Device Security Matters

Phones, tablets, and computers contain valuable information such as photos, messages, contacts, documents, passwords, and account information.

Protecting your devices helps reduce the chance of unauthorized access, data theft, and malware infections.

🔄 Keep Your Device Updated

Software updates often include security fixes that address vulnerabilities.

  • Update your operating system.
  • Update your applications.
  • Install security updates promptly.
  • Use automatic updates when appropriate.

🔒 Lock Your Devices

Always use a screen lock on your phone, tablet, or computer.

  • Use a strong PIN or password.
  • Use biometric security when available.
  • Set your device to lock automatically.
  • Never leave an unlocked device unattended.

⚠️ Be Careful With Applications

Applications can request access to information and device features.

  • Install apps from trusted sources.
  • Review permissions before granting access.
  • Remove applications you no longer need.
  • Be suspicious of apps requesting unnecessary permissions.

📍 Protect Your Privacy

Think carefully about the personal information you share online.

  • Avoid publicly sharing sensitive information.
  • Review privacy settings on social platforms.
  • Be careful when sharing your location.
  • Think before posting photos or documents.
  • Review who can see your information.

🚨 If Your Device Is Lost or Stolen

  1. Use your device's tracking or remote-lock feature if available.
  2. Change important account passwords.
  3. Contact your mobile provider when appropriate.
  4. Notify your organization if it is a work device.
  5. Report theft to the appropriate authorities when necessary.

💾 Back Up Important Information

Backups can help you recover important information if your device is damaged, lost, stolen, or affected by malware.

Keep important backups protected and make sure you can actually restore your files when needed.

✅ Device Security Checklist

  • Use a screen lock.
  • Keep software updated.
  • Install apps from trusted sources.
  • Review app permissions.
  • Protect personal information.
  • Back up important files.
  • Enable device tracking when available.
