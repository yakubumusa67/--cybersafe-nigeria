📧 Phishing Emails

⏱️ 10 Minutes 📖 Course 3 🎣 Phishing Awareness

What Is a Phishing Email?

A phishing email is a fraudulent message designed to look like it came from a legitimate person or organization. Its goal is usually to persuade you to click a link, open an attachment, or reveal sensitive information.

Common Warning Signs

  • 🚨 The message creates unnecessary urgency.
  • 🔗 The link leads to an unfamiliar website.
  • 📧 The sender's address looks unusual.
  • 🔐 The email asks for your password or financial information.
  • 🎁 You are unexpectedly told that you won a prize.
  • ✍️ The message contains strange spelling or grammar.
⚠️ Never trust an email just because it contains a familiar logo. Scammers can copy logos and imitate the appearance of legitimate organizations.

Before You Click a Link

Stop and check where the link actually goes. If possible, visit the organization's official website yourself instead of using the link inside the suspicious message.

🛡️ CyberSafe Tip: When an email asks you to act urgently, slow down. Verify the sender and the request before doing anything.
