Cybersecurity Best Practices

⏱ Estimated Time: 25 Minutes

🎓🛡️

Welcome to the Final Lesson!

You have reached the final lesson of Course 1.

This lesson brings together the most important cybersecurity habits you have learned.

🔐 1. Protect Your Accounts

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication whenever it is available.
  • Never share passwords or verification codes.
  • Review suspicious account activity.

🎣 2. Think Before You Click

Cybercriminals frequently use messages, links, attachments, and fake websites to trick people.

  • Check unexpected messages carefully.
  • Be suspicious of urgent requests.
  • Verify important requests independently.
  • Don't open suspicious attachments.
  • Don't enter sensitive information into suspicious websites.

📱 3. Protect Your Devices

  • Keep your operating system updated.
  • Install applications from trusted sources.
  • Use a screen lock.
  • Review application permissions.
  • Back up important information.

🌐 4. Stay Safe Online

  • Be careful when using public Wi-Fi.
  • Look for HTTPS when appropriate.
  • Protect your personal information.
  • Review your privacy settings.
  • Think carefully before posting information online.

🚨 5. Recognize Scams

Be careful when someone unexpectedly asks for money, passwords, verification codes, or sensitive information.

Common warning signs include:

  • Extreme urgency
  • Threats or fear
  • Promises that sound too good to be true
  • Requests for secrecy
  • Suspicious links
  • Unusual payment requests

🧠 The Cybersecurity Mindset

Cybersecurity is not only about technology. It is also about making good decisions.

Before you click, download, reply, pay, or share information, take a moment to think.

Stop. Think. Verify. Then act.
