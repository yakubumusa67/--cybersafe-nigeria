CYBER DICTIONARY

Learn Cybersecurity One Word at a Time

Welcome to CyberSafe Nigeria's Cyber Dictionary. Here you will learn important cybersecurity terms used by professionals around the world.

1. Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, devices, networks and information from cyber threats.

CyberSafe Tip: Cybersecurity begins with awareness and safe online habits.

2. Password

A password is a secret code used to protect your accounts and devices.

CyberSafe Tip: Never use the same password everywhere.

3. OTP

OTP means One-Time Password. It is a temporary code used to verify your identity.

CyberSafe Tip: Never share your OTP with anyone.

4. Phishing

Phishing is a scam used to trick people into revealing sensitive information.

CyberSafe Tip: Think before you click.

5. Malware

Malware is malicious software designed to damage or gain unauthorized access to systems.

Examples include viruses, spyware and ransomware.

6. Firewall

A firewall helps monitor and control network traffic to improve security.

7. Encryption

Encryption converts information into a protected format that can only be read by authorized parties.

8. VPN

VPN stands for Virtual Private Network. It helps create a protected connection while using networks.

9. Two-Factor Authentication

Two-Factor Authentication adds an additional layer of account security.

10. Digital Privacy

Digital privacy involves protecting your personal information online.

11. Spyware

Spyware is malicious software that secretly collects information from devices.

12. Social Engineering

Social engineering involves manipulating people into revealing sensitive information.

13. Data Breach

A data breach occurs when protected information is exposed without authorization.

14. Antivirus Software

Antivirus software helps detect and remove malicious programs from devices.

15. Ransomware

Ransomware is malware that prevents access to files or systems until a payment demand is addressed.

CyberSafe Tip: Regular backups can reduce the impact of ransomware incidents.

16. Browser Security

Browser security refers to practices that help protect your online activities while browsing the internet.

17. Cyber Awareness

Cyber awareness is understanding cyber risks and adopting safer digital practices.

18. Deepfake

Deepfakes are digitally manipulated audio, images or videos that can make content appear authentic when it is not.

19. Ethical Hacking

Ethical hacking involves authorized security testing to identify and help address vulnerabilities responsibly.

20. Cyber Crime

Cyber crime refers to unlawful activities that involve computers, networks or digital technologies.

Learning cybersecurity helps people recognize and avoid common online threats.
