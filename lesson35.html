🛡️ How to Stay Safe From Phishing

⏱️ 15 Minutes 📖 Course 3 🎣 Phishing Awareness 🏆 Final Lesson

🧠 Think Before You Click

Phishing attacks depend on deception. Attackers often try to make you panic, act quickly, or trust a message without checking whether it is genuine.

The best defense is to slow down and verify suspicious requests before taking action.

🛡️ Five Rules for Staying Safe

  • 1. Stop. Don't immediately click unexpected links.
  • 2. Check. Look carefully at the sender and website address.
  • 3. Verify. Contact the organization through a trusted channel.
  • 4. Protect. Never share passwords, PINs, or verification codes.
  • 5. Report. Report suspicious messages to the appropriate platform or organization.
⚠️ Remember:

Legitimate organizations generally won't need you to disclose your private password or authentication code through an unexpected message.

🚨 What If You Already Clicked?

Don't panic. Take action quickly.

  • Close the suspicious website.
  • Don't provide additional information.
  • If you entered a password, change it immediately.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication where available.
  • Monitor important accounts for unusual activity.
  • Report the incident through the appropriate channel.
🛡️ CyberSafe Rule:

STOP → CHECK → VERIFY → PROTECT → REPORT
