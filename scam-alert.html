🚨 CyberSafe Nigeria Scam Alert Center

Stay Informed. Stay Protected.

Cybercriminals constantly develop new ways to trick people into revealing personal information or sending money. This page helps you recognize common scams and explains how to respond safely.

🚨 1. Phishing Scams

Attackers send fake emails, text messages or social media messages pretending to be banks, schools or trusted organizations to steal passwords, OTPs and other personal information.

Warning Signs

  • Urgent requests to act immediately.
  • Requests for passwords or OTPs.
  • Suspicious links.
  • Poor grammar and spelling.

💰 2. Investment Scams

Scammers promise guaranteed profits or extremely high returns with little or no risk.

Warning Signs

  • "Double your money."
  • "Guaranteed profits."
  • Pressure to invest immediately.
  • Requests for cryptocurrency or gift cards.

💼 3. Fake Job Scams

Scammers advertise fake jobs and ask applicants to pay for training, registration or equipment before starting work.

Stay Safe

  • Research the company.
  • Never pay to apply for a legitimate job.
  • Verify job offers using official company contacts.

❤️ 4. Romance Scams

Scammers build fake online relationships to gain trust before asking for money or personal information.

Stay Safe

  • Be cautious with online relationships.
  • Avoid sending money to people you have not met.
  • Watch for inconsistent stories.

📱 5. Social Media Scams

Fake giveaways, impersonation accounts and fraudulent investment opportunities are common on social media.

Stay Safe

  • Verify official accounts.
  • Ignore unrealistic offers.
  • Report fake profiles.

🏦 6. Banking Scams

Criminals pretend to represent banks and request your PIN, password or One-Time Password (OTP).

Remember

  • Never share your PIN.
  • Never share your OTP.
  • Contact your bank through official channels if you're unsure.

🛡 CyberSafe Safety Checklist

  • ✔ Think before you click.
  • ✔ Verify unexpected messages.
  • ✔ Use strong, unique passwords.
  • ✔ Enable Two-Factor Authentication.
  • ✔ Keep your devices updated.
  • ✔ Report suspicious activity.

Emergency Reminder

If you believe you've been targeted by a scam, stop communicating with the scammer, secure your accounts by changing passwords where appropriate, and contact your bank or the affected service using its official support channels if financial or account information may be at risk.
