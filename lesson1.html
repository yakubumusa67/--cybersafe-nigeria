Introduction to Cybersecurity

⏱ Estimated Time: 15 Minutes

What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, smartphones, networks and digital information from cyber threats, attacks and unauthorized access. It helps individuals, businesses and governments keep their systems secure and their data safe.

Why Cybersecurity Matters

  • 🛡 Protects personal information.
  • 💳 Prevents financial fraud.
  • 🏢 Secures businesses.
  • 🌍 Protects national infrastructure.
  • 📱 Keeps devices safe from malware.

The CIA Triad

Confidentiality

Only authorized people should access sensitive information.

Integrity

Information should remain accurate and unchanged unless authorized.

Availability

Systems and information should always be available when needed.
