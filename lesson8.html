Basic Network Security

⏱ Estimated Time: 20 Minutes

🌐 What Is a Network?

A network is a group of connected devices that can communicate and share information or resources.

Networks can include computers, smartphones, servers, printers, routers, and other devices.

🛡️ Why Network Security Matters

Network security helps protect devices, information, and communication from unauthorized access and other security threats.

Good network security is important at home, in schools, businesses, and public environments.

📡 Secure Your Wi-Fi

  • Use a strong Wi-Fi password.
  • Change default administrator passwords on your router when appropriate.
  • Keep your router's firmware updated.
  • Use modern Wi-Fi security such as WPA2 or WPA3 when supported.
  • Avoid sharing your Wi-Fi password publicly.

⚠️ Public Wi-Fi

Public Wi-Fi can be convenient, but you should still be careful when using it.

  • Confirm that you are connecting to the correct network.
  • Avoid entering sensitive information on suspicious websites or networks.
  • Keep your device's security protections enabled.
  • Use HTTPS websites when entering information.
  • Disconnect from networks you no longer need.

🔒 What Is HTTPS?

HTTPS helps protect information exchanged between your browser and a website by encrypting the connection.

Look for the lock symbol and https:// when visiting websites, especially before entering sensitive information.

Remember: HTTPS helps protect the connection, but it does not automatically mean that a website itself is trustworthy.

🔥 Firewalls

A firewall is a security control that can monitor and control network traffic based on defined rules.

Firewalls can help reduce unauthorized network access, but they are only one part of a complete cybersecurity strategy.

🚨 Warning Signs

  • Unknown devices connected to your network.
  • Unexpected changes to router settings.
  • Unusual network activity.
  • Your Wi-Fi password suddenly stops working.
  • Security warnings that you cannot explain.

✅ Network Security Checklist

  • Use a strong Wi-Fi password.
  • Update your router regularly.
  • Secure your devices.
  • Be careful on public Wi-Fi.
  • Use HTTPS when appropriate.
  • Keep firewall protections enabled where appropriate.
  • Monitor unfamiliar devices on your network.
