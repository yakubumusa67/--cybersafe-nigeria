🛡️ Password Managers & Multi-Factor Authentication

⏱️ Estimated Time: 10 Minutes

🎓 Final Lesson of Course 2

You made it to the final lesson!

Now let's learn two powerful ways to strengthen your account security: Password Managers and Multi-Factor Authentication.

🔐 What Is a Password Manager?

A password manager is a tool that can securely store your passwords and help you create strong, unique passwords for different accounts.

Instead of trying to remember dozens of different passwords, you can use a password manager to manage them more securely.

✅ Benefits of Password Managers

  • Help create strong passwords.
  • Help store unique passwords for different accounts.
  • Reduce the need to remember many passwords.
  • Can help identify weak or reused passwords.
  • Can make secure password practices easier.

🛡️ What Is Multi-Factor Authentication?

Multi-Factor Authentication, commonly called MFA, adds another verification step when you sign in to an account.

Instead of relying only on a password, MFA can require another factor to verify that it is really you.

🔑 Common Authentication Factors

  • Something you know: such as a password or PIN.
  • Something you have: such as an authentication device or security key.
  • Something you are: such as a fingerprint or facial recognition.

⚠️ Never Share Verification Codes

Treat verification codes like passwords.

If someone contacts you and asks for a login verification code, be extremely cautious.

A legitimate support representative should not need you to disclose a private authentication code.

🧠 Password Security Checklist

  • Use long, unique passwords.
  • Don't reuse important passwords.
  • Consider using a reputable password manager.
  • Enable MFA on important accounts.
  • Never share passwords or verification codes.
  • Review your accounts regularly.
