REPORT A CYBER SCAM

If you encounter suspicious messages, phishing attempts, or online scams, do not share your passwords, OTPs or banking information.

SAFETY REMINDERS

Protect Your Passwords

Never share your passwords with anyone.

Enable 2FA

Use Two-Factor Authentication whenever possible.

Verify Before Clicking

Be cautious of suspicious links, emails and attachments.

Stay Updated

Keep your devices and applications updated for improved security.
