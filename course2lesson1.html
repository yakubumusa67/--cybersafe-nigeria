🔐 Creating Strong Passwords

⏱️ Estimated Time: 10 Minutes

🔑 What Makes a Password Strong?

A strong password makes it much harder for someone to guess or break into your account.

Good passwords should be long, unique, and difficult for other people to predict.

✅ Characteristics of a Strong Password

  • Use a long password or passphrase.
  • Use a different password for each important account.
  • Mix letters, numbers, and symbols when appropriate.
  • Avoid information that people can easily guess.
  • Never use passwords such as "123456" or "password".

⚠️ Avoid These Password Mistakes

  • Using your name as a password.
  • Using your birthday.
  • Using the same password everywhere.
  • Sharing your password with other people.
  • Writing passwords where anyone can see them.
  • Using simple patterns such as 123456.

🧠 Password Example

Instead of using a predictable password like:

password123

consider using a long, unique passphrase that is difficult to guess.

Never use an example password from a lesson as your real password.

🔒 Why Unique Passwords Matter

Imagine using the same password for your email, social media, and banking account.

If one service suffers a breach and your password is exposed, attackers may try that same password on your other accounts.

Using unique passwords limits the damage if one account is compromised.
