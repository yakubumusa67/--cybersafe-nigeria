Password & Account Security

⏱ Estimated Time: 20 Minutes

Why Password Security Matters

Your password is one of the first barriers protecting your online accounts. If an attacker obtains your password, they may be able to access your account, personal information, messages, or other services.

A strong password is important, but good account security involves more than just creating a complicated password.

🔐 Avoid Weak Passwords

Examples of passwords that are easy to guess include:

password123
12345678
qwerty
yourname123

Avoid passwords based on easily available information such as your name, birthday, phone number, or favorite team.

✅ Create Strong Passwords

  • Use long passwords or passphrases.
  • Use a different password for each important account.
  • Avoid predictable personal information.
  • Consider using a reputable password manager.
  • Never share your passwords with other people.

A password manager can help you create and securely store unique passwords so you don't have to memorize every one.

🛡️ Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) adds another security layer to an account.

Instead of relying only on a password, the service may require another form of verification, such as an authenticator app, security key, or another approved authentication method.

Whenever available, enabling MFA can significantly strengthen account security.

⚠️ Protect Verification Codes

One-time passwords and verification codes are sensitive. Someone asking you to send them a code may be attempting to take control of your account.

Never give a verification code to someone who contacts you unexpectedly.

🚨 If Your Password Is Compromised

  1. Change the password immediately.
  2. Use a new password that you haven't used elsewhere.
  3. Enable MFA if it is available.
  4. Review recent account activity.
  5. Sign out of unfamiliar sessions or devices.
  6. Check whether other accounts reused the same password.

🔑 Golden Rules

  • Unique passwords are better than reused passwords.
  • Long passwords are generally harder to guess.
  • MFA provides an additional layer of protection.
  • Never share passwords or authentication codes.
  • Use official websites and apps when changing account settings.
