ABOUT CYBERSAFE NIGERIA

CyberSafe Nigeria is a cybersecurity education platform dedicated to helping Nigerians stay safe online through free, practical and accessible cybersecurity training.

OUR MISSION

To provide FREE and practical cybersecurity education for every Nigerian regardless of age or background.

OUR VISION

Education

Making cybersecurity knowledge accessible to everyone.

Awareness

Helping Nigerians identify and avoid cyber threats.

Protection

Promoting safer online practices for individuals and businesses.
