About CyberSafe

About CyberSafe

CyberSafe is an educational cybersecurity platform created to help people understand online security, cyber threats, scams, privacy, passwords, and responsible technology use.

Our goal is to make cybersecurity easier to understand for everyone, from beginners to people who want to develop stronger cybersecurity knowledge.

My Story

My name is Yakubu Musa, the founder and designer of CyberSafe.

I created CyberSafe because cybersecurity is becoming increasingly important in our everyday lives.

People use the internet for communication, banking, education, business, entertainment, and many other activities. CyberSafe was created to educate people about online dangers and encourage safer, smarter, and more responsible use of technology.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide simple, practical, and accessible cybersecurity education while helping people recognize scams, protect their accounts, create stronger passwords, and understand common cyber threats.
