📱 Smishing & Social Media Phishing

⏱️ 10 Minutes 📖 Course 3 🎣 Phishing Awareness

What Is Smishing?

Smishing is phishing carried out through SMS or other text messages. The word combines "SMS" and "phishing."

A scammer may send a message pretending to be a bank, delivery company, mobile provider, online service, or another organization.

🚩 Common Smishing Warning Signs

  • 🚨 A message demands immediate action.
  • 🔗 It contains an unexpected link.
  • 💳 It asks for payment or financial information.
  • 🔐 It asks for a password or verification code.
  • 🎁 It claims you have won an unexpected reward.
  • 📦 It claims there is a problem with a delivery.
⚠️ Never share a verification code just because someone asks for it. If you receive an unexpected security message, verify it through the official service.

📲 Social Media Phishing

Phishing can also happen through social media platforms. Attackers may create fake profiles or pretend to be friends, businesses, celebrities, or support staff.

They may send links, ask for personal information, or try to move conversations to another platform.

👤 Fake Accounts & Impersonation

  • Check whether an account is genuine.
  • Be suspicious of newly created accounts.
  • Don't trust unexpected requests for money.
  • Don't share passwords or security codes.
  • Verify unusual requests through another trusted channel.
🛡️ CyberSafe Tip: If a friend suddenly messages you asking for money or sensitive information, contact them through another trusted method before responding.

🧠 Remember

Phishing isn't limited to email. It can appear in text messages, social media, messaging apps, and other communication channels.

The same rule applies everywhere: Stop. Think. Verify.
