🔗 Fake Links & Websites

⏱️ 10 Minutes 📖 Course 3 🎣 Phishing Awareness

Why Do Fake Links Matter?

Phishing attacks often use links that appear legitimate but actually lead to fraudulent websites. These websites may imitate banks, social media platforms, online stores, or other trusted services.

The goal is usually to convince you to enter information such as usernames, passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

🚩 Signs of a Suspicious Link

  • 🔗 The domain name looks unfamiliar or slightly different.
  • 🔤 The website address contains unexpected spelling changes.
  • 🚨 The message pressures you to click immediately.
  • 🎁 The link promises an unexpected prize or reward.
  • 🔐 The page suddenly asks for sensitive information.
  • 📩 The link arrived unexpectedly from an unknown sender.
⚠️ Be careful with look-alike websites. A website can be designed to look almost identical to a legitimate service while being controlled by someone else.

🌐 How to Check a Website

Before entering sensitive information, look carefully at the website address and make sure you are on the service's legitimate website.

If you receive an unexpected message asking you to log in, consider opening the official app or typing the organization's known website address yourself instead of following the message's link.

🛡️ CyberSafe Tip: Don't let urgency make the decision for you. Stop, verify the website, and only then continue.

🧠 Remember

A familiar logo, professional design, or convincing message does not prove that a website is genuine.

Always verify before entering sensitive information.
