Phishing: Recognizing Online Scams

⏱ Estimated Time: 20 Minutes

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyberattack in which an attacker pretends to be a trustworthy person, company, or service in order to trick someone into revealing information or taking an unsafe action.

Phishing can happen through email, text messages, social media, phone calls, or fake websites.

🎣 Common Phishing Examples

  • A fake bank message asking you to verify your account.
  • A fake delivery message containing a suspicious link.
  • A fake social-media login page asking for your password.
  • A message pretending to be your boss requesting money.
  • A fake prize notification asking for personal information.

⚠️ Warning Signs of Phishing

  • Unexpected messages.
  • Urgent or threatening language.
  • Suspicious links.
  • Requests for passwords or verification codes.
  • Requests for money or financial information.
  • Unusual sender addresses or phone numbers.
  • Spelling or grammar that seems unusual.
  • Offers that seem too good to be true.

🔗 Check Links Before Clicking

Attackers may create links that look similar to legitimate websites. Before opening a link, check where it actually leads and whether the domain belongs to the organization you expect.

When in doubt, open the organization's official website yourself instead of using the link in the message.

🛡️ How to Protect Yourself

  • Do not rush because a message says you must act immediately.
  • Never share passwords or one-time verification codes.
  • Verify unexpected requests through another trusted channel.
  • Do not open suspicious attachments.
  • Use multi-factor authentication.
  • Report suspicious messages.
  • Keep your devices and applications updated.

🚨 What If You Clicked a Phishing Link?

Don't panic. Stop interacting with the suspicious page and avoid entering additional information.

If you entered a password, change it immediately from the legitimate service and review your account security. If financial information was exposed, contact your bank or financial institution through an official channel.
