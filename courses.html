Course 3: Phishing Awareness

Welcome to Course 3 of the CyberSafe Nigeria Cybersecurity Program. In this lesson, you will learn what phishing is, how cybercriminals use it to steal information, and the steps you can take to protect yourself.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand what phishing is.
  • Identify different types of phishing attacks.
  • Recognize warning signs of phishing.
  • Learn how to respond safely.
  • Protect your personal and financial information.

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where criminals pretend to be trusted people or organizations to trick victims into revealing sensitive information such as passwords, bank details or verification codes.

Information Attackers Try to Steal

  • Passwords
  • Bank account details
  • ATM card information
  • One-Time Passwords (OTP)
  • Email accounts
  • Social media accounts
  • Personal identity information

Types of Phishing

  • Email Phishing
  • SMS Phishing (Smishing)
  • Voice Phishing (Vishing)
  • Social Media Phishing
  • Fake Website Phishing
  • QR Code Phishing

Warning Signs

  • Messages creating urgency.
  • Requests for passwords or OTPs.
  • Suspicious website addresses.
  • Poor grammar and spelling.
  • Unexpected prizes or rewards.
  • Unknown attachments.

Example of a Phishing Message

"Congratulations! You have won ₦500,000. Click this link immediately and enter your bank details to receive your reward."

This is a common scam. Always verify unexpected offers through official channels before responding.

How to Protect Yourself

  • Never share your password.
  • Never share your OTP.
  • Verify links before clicking.
  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication.
  • Keep your device updated.
  • Think before you click.
  • Use trusted websites only.
  • Report suspicious messages.

Real-Life Scenario

You receive an email claiming to be from your bank asking you to "confirm your account immediately" by clicking a link. Instead of clicking, open your bank's official app or website yourself, or contact the bank through its official customer service number to verify the request.

Course Summary

  • Phishing is a common online scam.
  • Attackers often pretend to be trusted organizations.
  • Never share passwords or OTPs.
  • Always verify suspicious messages.
  • Use strong security practices every day.
