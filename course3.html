🎣 Introduction to Phishing

⏱️ 10 Minutes 📖 Course 3 🎓 Phishing Awareness

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of cybercrime where attackers pretend to be trustworthy people, companies, banks, websites or organizations in order to trick people into revealing sensitive information.

The attacker may use an email, text message, phone call, social media message or fake website to make the victim believe that the request is legitimate.

What Information Do Attackers Want?

  • 🔑 Passwords
  • 💳 Bank or payment information
  • 📧 Email account details
  • 📱 Phone numbers and personal information
  • 🔐 Login credentials
⚠️ Important: A message that looks official does not automatically mean that it is genuine. Always verify suspicious requests before clicking links or providing information.

Common Signs of Phishing

  • 🚨 Messages creating a sense of urgency
  • 🔗 Suspicious or unfamiliar links
  • ✍️ Spelling or grammar mistakes
  • 🎁 Unexpected prizes or rewards
  • 🔐 Requests for passwords or sensitive information
  • 👤 Messages pretending to be someone you know
🛡️ CyberSafe Tip: Stop and verify before you click. If a message asks you to provide sensitive information urgently, treat it with caution.
